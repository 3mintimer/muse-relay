# Muse Relay 0.4.1

Reply silently from G2. On the last page of Muse's answer, tap to see three suggested replies and tap the one you want to send. In the glasses chat's contextual menu, switch between one message and input with its reply, let Muse suggest replies when useful, ask every time, or turn suggestions off.

Open Even Hub in the Even Realities app and search for **Muse Relay** to install or update it. Ordinary chat works with your current relay. Suggestions start off when upgrading from 0.3.x. [Even Hub listing](https://hub.evenrealities.com/catalogue/com.threemintimer.muserelay) · [Enable suggestions for your relay](https://muse-relay-guide.spicy-cup-4319.chatgpt.site/#reply-suggestions-upgrade)

## 日本語

G2でMuseの回答を最後まで読み、タップして開いた三択から、そのまま声を使わずに返信できます。チャットのコンテクスチュアルメニューから「入力と回答」「1件ずつ」の表示切り替えや、返信候補の「オフ」「Museに任せる」「毎回候補を依頼」を選べます。

Even Realities アプリの「Even Hub」で **Muse Relay** を検索してインストールまたは更新できます。普段の会話には、これまでの中継をそのまま使えます。0.3 系から更新した場合、返信候補の初期設定はオフです。[ストア掲載ページ](https://hub.evenrealities.com/catalogue/com.threemintimer.muserelay) · [接続方式別の返信候補の設定](https://muse-relay-guide.spicy-cup-4319.chatgpt.site/ja/#reply-suggestions-upgrade)
