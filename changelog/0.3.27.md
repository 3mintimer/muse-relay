# Muse Relay 0.3.27

- Share your phone’s recent location with Muse from the chat toolbar or Settings. Sharing starts off. Sending works immediately, including from G2 while the phone screen is hidden.
- When available, a location less than five minutes old travels separately from the message text. The reference HTTPS relay forwards it as an attachment and the Gist watcher supplies it to your handler. Custom relays can read the optional `location` field.

## 日本語

- チャット欄の「現在地」または設定から、母艦スマートフォンの位置を共有できます。初期設定はオフです。
- G2 からの送信も位置取得を待ちません。5分以内の位置があるときだけ、本文と別に中継へ渡します。
